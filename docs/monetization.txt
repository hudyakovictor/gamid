## Current rule

The canonical payment model is:

```text
Telegram Stars/XTR
→ Coin Pack
→ Coins
→ Signal Arena goods and services
```

Regular products are not directly purchased with Telegram Stars. Direct Stars entitlement is an exceptional, separately announced platform or promotion path with its own SKU, feature flag, reconciliation and approval gates.

Use the following documents instead:

- `economy_monetization_referrals.md` for economy behavior, Coin ledger, referrals and anti-pay-to-win rules;
- `game_balance_spec.md` for XP, Account Level, Mastery Stars, Energy and balance hypotheses;
- `catalog_sku_spec.md` for Founder Packs, SKU composition, prices, entitlements, assets and refund/revoke rules;
- `topbar_currency_ui_spec.md` for display only.

## Historical migration notes

The former direct-Stars catalog terminology (`Paid for Stars`, direct Stars product checkout, `rematch token` and `non-ranked attempt`) is deprecated. Do not copy those terms into new UI, contracts, analytics events or code.

Rewarded ads now support only:

```text
ENERGY_GRANT
COSMETIC_FRAGMENT_GRANT
```

One completed, server-verified rewarded ad grants `+1 Energy`, subject to daily/session caps. It grants no direct XP, Mastery Stars, rating, score modifier, tournament access or hidden information. Scheduled Rematch is free; immediate voluntary Rematch uses Energy.
